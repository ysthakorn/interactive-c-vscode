AX-11 Activity Board — Firmware Download

Step 1: Connect your AX-11 board to the computer using a serial cable (RS-232) or USB-to-Serial adapter.

Make sure the serial cable is securely connected to both the AX-11 board and the computer's serial port.
If using a USB-to-Serial adapter, ensure the driver is installed and the correct COM port is selected.

Step 2: Click on the "Communication Check" button and see if the serial light on the board blinks.

If it does not blink, there is probably either a problem with the serial connection, or the wrong serial port has been chosen.
Check all serial connections, ensure that you've chosen the correct serial port, and try again.

Step 3: Make sure the AX-11 board is switched OFF.

Slide the power switch to the OFF position before proceeding.

Step 4: While holding the STOP button, turn the power ON.

Keep the STOP button pressed for at least two seconds after power is turned on.
This puts the board into bootstrap/download mode.

Step 5: Both the RED BATT and GREEN PWR lights should be off (after shining momentarily).

  • If the BATT light stays on, your battery is too low. Turn off and charge for 30 minutes.
  • If the PWR light stays on, the board did not enter download mode. Go back to Step 3 and try again.

If both lights are off, click the Download Firmware button to begin downloading.

Step 6: Wait for the firmware download to complete.

Do NOT disconnect the cable or turn off the board during download.
The progress bar will show the download status.
When complete, turn the board OFF and then ON again to start using the new firmware.
